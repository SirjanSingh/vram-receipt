A farewell letter

Dear Jensen, thank you.

You started Nvidia in a Denny's booth in 1993 and led it for thirty-three years. On November 12, 2026, you stepped down.

Every game we stayed up too late for, every model we trained on a gaming card at 2 a.m., ran on something you built.

So here's our goodbye card: everyone's VRAM, pooled in one place.

With love and a few gigabytes, every chip in the cluster
