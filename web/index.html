How much VRAM did each GeForce generation give you per launch dollar?

Every card's launch MSRP, traced to the Nvidia page that announced it, then adjusted for inflation. Get a receipt for your own GPU and RAM, then take the data.

npx vram-receipt

Runs on your machine and uploads nothing. Prices are launch MSRP, not street price.

Before you quote a number

        
    
          
  • MSRP is not what people paid. Many of these cards never sold at list price. No price history exists that can be legally republished, so there is none here.
    • 
          
  • Blank means unknown. A card with no confirmed primary-source price has no price, not a guess.
    • 
          
  • Desktop cards only. Laptop GPUs have no list price. The command still shows their VRAM and where it sits on the Steam survey.
